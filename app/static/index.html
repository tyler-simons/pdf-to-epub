How Rebind works+

  1. Bring your PDF

    Upload a book, paper, or document. Rebind extracts its text and uses character recognition for scanned pages.

  2. Give it room to flow

    We package the text into an EPUB, so your reader can adjust its type and layout. Complex equations and tables may stay as page images to preserve their meaning.

  3. Make yourself comfortable

    Download your EPUB and open it in Apple Books, or use Send to Kindle. Choose your font size and reading theme in your reader.

A little care, a few limits

When AI cleanup is on, a small selection of text with suspected recognition errors is sent to OpenAI for correction. This is a light cleanup, not a full proofread. Image-based pages won’t have the same adjustable text as the rest of the book.

Files are stored privately and expire after 24 hours. Keep your downloaded copy. Large or heavily scanned books may need to be split into smaller sections.
