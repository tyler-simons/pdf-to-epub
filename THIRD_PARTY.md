# Third-party dependencies

This project uses packages installed separately from requirements.txt. Their licenses continue to apply.

- PyMuPDF / MuPDF: AGPL-3.0 or Artifex commercial license; https://pymupdf.readthedocs.io/en/latest/about.html
- FastAPI, Starlette, Uvicorn, python-multipart: respective upstream open-source licenses.
- Pillow: HPND-style license; https://pillow.readthedocs.io/en/stable/about.html
- Tesseract OCR: Apache-2.0; https://github.com/tesseract-ocr/tesseract

No OCR model weights, third-party book content, keys, or proprietary fonts are bundled.

Self-hosted typography: Manrope and DM Sans, distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Their full licenses are included beside the fonts in `app/static/fonts/`.
